Return Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Not happy with something you bought on {{business_trade_name}}? In most cases you can return it within 14 days of delivery.

1. Return window

You can request a return for up to 14 days from the date the item was delivered. Some categories have a different window; the exact return period, or a note that the item can't be returned, is shown on the product page and in your order details.

2. Conditions

To be accepted, a returned item must be:

  • unused, unwashed and in the same condition you received it;
  • in its original packaging, with all tags, accessories, manuals, free gifts and seals included;
  • accompanied by the order number.

These conditions don't apply in the same way to items that arrived damaged, defective or wrong; please report those as soon as possible, ideally within 48 hours of delivery, with photos.

3. Items that can't be returned

Unless they arrived damaged, defective or wrong, the following can't be returned for hygiene, safety or nature-of-product reasons:

  • opened beauty, personal-care and fragrance products;
  • underwear, swimwear and pierced jewellery;
  • food, groceries and other perishable goods;
  • opened software, video games, digital codes and gift cards;
  • products customised or made to order;
  • any product marked "non-returnable" on its product page.

4. How to return an item

  1. Sign in and go to My Account → My Orders.
  2. Open the order, select the item(s) and quantity, and choose Return.
  3. Pick a reason (for example wrong item, damaged, not as described, size issue, changed my mind) and add photos if the item is damaged or wrong.
  4. Choose what you'd like: a refund, an exchange or store credit.
  5. Confirm the pickup address. Our courier will contact you to collect the item; please pack it securely.

An order from several sellers may produce one return per seller; you can follow each one in My Orders.

5. Inspection and outcome

Once the item reaches the seller it is inspected. If it meets the conditions above, your return is approved and your refund, exchange or store credit is processed as described in our Refund Policy. If it doesn't, we'll explain why and send the item back to you.

6. Return shipping

  • Our or the seller's mistake (wrong, damaged, defective or not-as-described item): returning it is free and you receive a full refund, including the original delivery fee.
  • Change of mind or wrong size: pickup is arranged for you, but the original delivery fee is refunded only if you return the whole shipment. See the Refund Policy for details.

7. Cancel instead of return

If your order hasn't shipped yet, you don't need to return it: just cancel it from My Orders and you'll be refunded in full.
