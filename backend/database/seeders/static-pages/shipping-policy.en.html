Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This policy explains where we deliver, how long delivery takes and how much it costs.

1. Where we deliver

We deliver to addresses across the Sultanate of Oman, including Muscat, Salalah, Sohar and other governorates. Options for your area are confirmed at checkout once you choose your delivery address. If an item can't be delivered to your address, you'll see this before you pay.

2. Delivery options and times

OptionEstimated delivery
Standard Delivery2–4 business days
Express Delivery1–2 business days

Where available for your address and items, Same-Day Delivery, Scheduled Delivery or Click & Collect may also be offered at checkout. The options available depend on the item, the seller's location and your address. Each product page and the checkout show an estimated delivery date. Times are counted from order confirmation; orders placed after the daily cut-off, or on weekends and public holidays, are processed on the next business day.

3. Delivery fees

  • The delivery fee for each shipment is calculated from the delivery option, your address and the size and weight of the items, and is always shown at checkout before you pay.
  • Free delivery applies to eligible orders above the threshold shown at checkout or on the product page.
  • Prices include VAT where applicable.

4. Orders from several sellers

If your order contains items from different sellers or warehouses, it may arrive in several packages on different days. Each package has its own tracking.

5. Tracking your order

We'll notify you by email, SMS/WhatsApp and in the app when your order is confirmed, shipped, out for delivery and delivered. You can track every shipment at any time in My Account → My Orders.

6. Receiving your order

  • Our courier will call you before arriving, so please keep your phone available.
  • For Cash on Delivery orders (up to OMR 300), please have the exact amount ready.
  • Please check the package when you receive it. If it is visibly damaged or opened, you may refuse it, or report it to us within 48 hours with photos.

7. Failed delivery

If we can't reach you or deliver to your address after repeated attempts, the shipment is returned to the seller and the order is cancelled. Any amount you paid for it is refunded in line with our Refund Policy.

8. Delays

Delivery times are estimates. Occasionally, weather, public holidays, high demand or other events outside our control may cause delays. If your order is late, we'll keep you updated, and you can contact us at any time.
