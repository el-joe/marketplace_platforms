Refund Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This policy explains when you are entitled to a refund on {{business_trade_name}}, how much is refunded, and how and when the money reaches you.

1. When you get a refund

  • You cancel an order before it ships: full refund of the cancelled items.
  • The seller or {{business_trade_name}} cancels your order (for example, the item is out of stock): full refund.
  • Your return is approved under our Return Policy: refund of the returned items once they pass inspection.
  • Your order could not be delivered and was returned to the seller: refund of the undelivered items.

2. How much is refunded

  • Wrong, damaged, defective or not-as-described items, and orders cancelled by us or the seller: we refund the full amount you paid for the items, including delivery fees.
  • Change of mind or wrong size: we refund the amount you paid for the returned items. Delivery fees are refunded only if you return every item in that shipment, and a share of the card payment processing fee may be deducted for card payments.
  • Discounts and coupons are taken into account: you get back what you actually paid for the item, not its original price.

3. How you receive the money

Wallet balance can be spent on any future order. If you paid by Cash on Delivery and would prefer a bank transfer, contact us and our team will arrange it after verifying your details.

If you chose store credit when requesting a return, the refund always goes to your wallet.

4. Timeline

  1. Cancellations: the refund starts as soon as the cancellation is confirmed.
  2. Returns: after we collect the item, the seller inspects it on arrival. The refund is issued as soon as the return is approved.
  3. You get an email and in-app notification at each step, and you can follow the status in My Orders.

5. Refund not received?

Card refunds can take a few days to appear depending on your bank. If you haven't received it after 14 business days, contact us with your order number at {{business_support_email}} and we'll send you the refund reference to share with your bank.

6. Disputes

If you disagree with a refund decision, you can open a dispute from the order page and our team will review it. This does not affect your rights under the Consumer Protection Law of the Sultanate of Oman.
