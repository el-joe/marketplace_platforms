We process personal data in line with the Personal Data Protection Law of the Sultanate of Oman (Royal Decree 6/2022).

1. Data we collect

  • Account details: name, email address, mobile number, password (stored encrypted), language preference.
  • Order and delivery details: delivery addresses, items ordered, order history, returns, reviews and support conversations.
  • Payment details: card payments are processed by our licensed payment gateway, Thawani. We receive only a payment reference and limited card details (such as card type), never your full card number or CVV.
  • Device and usage data: IP address, browser and device type, pages viewed, and cookies, used to keep you signed in, remember your cart and improve the Platform.
  • Referral and marketing data: if you arrive through a referral or influencer link, we record that link to credit the referrer.

2. How we use your data

  • to create and manage your account;
  • to process, deliver and track your orders, returns, refunds and warranty claims;
  • to send order updates by email, SMS, WhatsApp and in-app notifications;
  • to provide customer support;
  • to prevent fraud and keep the Platform secure;
  • to improve our services and show relevant products;
  • to send marketing messages and newsletters, only where you have agreed. You can unsubscribe at any time;
  • to meet our legal, tax and accounting obligations.

3. Who we share it with

We never sell your personal data. We share only what is necessary with:

  • Sellers on the Platform: your name, delivery address and phone number, so they can fulfil your order;
  • Delivery partners, to deliver your orders and collect returns;
  • Payment providers (Thawani), to process payments and refunds;
  • Service providers who host our systems or send messages for us, under confidentiality obligations;
  • Authorities, where required by law.

4. Cookies

We use essential cookies to run the Platform (for example to keep you signed in and remember your cart) and analytics cookies to understand how it is used. You can control cookies in your browser settings; blocking essential cookies may stop some features from working.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is active and afterwards only as long as needed to meet legal, tax and accounting requirements or to resolve disputes.

6. Security

The whole Platform uses encrypted HTTPS connections. Passwords are stored encrypted, and access to personal data is limited to staff and partners who need it.
